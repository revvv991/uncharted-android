# Patches

- `fex-*.patch`: FEX (`~/src/fex`, branch `g2-android` on top of FEX `f2b679f`), MIT license:
  0001: the Android build of FEXCore and Common (bionic, libc++), a FEXCore-only target; 0002: the
  guest's pause is a `wfe` (`BB_PAUSE=yield`: a yield); 0003: that target without a smoke executable.
- `mesa-*.patch`: Mesa/Turnip (`~/src/mesa`, branch `g2-kgsl` on top of Mesa 26.2.5), MIT license:
  0001: polling timestamps instead of waiting with a zero timeout (fixed the same way upstream,
  e984ef294ea); 0002: u_trace gate; 0003: diagnostics: `TU_BO_LOG=1` (BO log with addresses,
  together with `TU_DEBUG=bos`), `TU_KGSL_CTX_FLAGS` (KGSL context flags); 0004: diagnostics:
  `TU_MEMCHECK=1` (a background thread compares pipeline and shader memory with its contents 2 s
  after creation and prints changes with GPU addresses); 0005: experiment `TU_DS_NO_CROSS=1` (state
  groups do not cross a page; did not help); 0006: experiment `TU_IB_ALIGN=<bytes>`:
  `CP_SET_DRAW_STATE` and `CP_(NON_)CONTEXT_REG_BUNCH` packets in IB1/IB2 do not cross block
  boundaries; 0007: the same for `CP_LOAD_STATE6*`, 1 KB by default; 0008: experiment `TU_BR_SYNC=1`
  (BV waits for BR in sysmem passes); 0009: experiment `TU_CS_FILL_NOP=1` (new command BOs filled with
  `CP_NOP`); 0010: experiment `TU_IB2_SPLIT=<dwords>` (draw IB2s are cut at draw boundaries); 0011:
  alignment off by default again (it only moved the A735 CP faults); 0012: IB2 cut every 256 dwords
  by default (`TU_IB2_SPLIT=0` turns it off); 0013: **recovery after a lost KGSL context**
  (`TU_KGSL_RECOVER`, on by default: after a series of GPU faults the queue moves to a new context
  and the game goes on; check with `TU_KGSL_FAULT_TEST=<s>`); 0014: **the command streams that become
  IBs leave the second 4 KiB page of their BOs unused** (on by default, `TU_PAGE1_SKIP=0` turns it
  off): the A735 CP faults sat in that page; 0015: the variables of 0003-0014 also come from Android
  properties (`debug.mesa.<name>`, `TU_PAGE1_SKIP` is `debug.mesa.tu.page1.skip`; the environment
  wins), and the notes about them and the recoveries also go to the Android log: for other apps that
  load this driver through libadrenotools; 0016: upstream's 118ec7f4 (Connor Abbott, after 26.2),
  meant to keep command BOs below 4 GB, as all known CP firmwares can fetch wrong IB contents after a
  preemption when an IB crosses a 4 GB boundary; on KGSL it does nothing (upstream too): its
  `KGSL_MEMFLAGS_FORCE_32BIT` is 1 << 32 and `kgsl_gpumem_alloc_id` has 32-bit flags; 0017: **those
  BOs come from `kgsl_gpuobj_alloc`, whose flags carry it, so the command streams do go below 4 GB**
  (`TU_IB_LOW4G=0` turns it off; without room there the usual allocation): with 0012 and 0014 off,
  6 U3 processes with it had no CP fault, 3 of 6 without it had 56 (2026-10-30); 0012 and 0014 stay;
  0022: **the Adreno 825** (KGSL chip id 0x44030000, Snapdragon 8s Gen 4; without it "No physical
  devices found"): the a830's entry (a8xx_gen1) with 2 slices and 4 CCUs - KGSL reports 2432 KiB of
  GMEM, exactly the a8xx_gen1 sysmem CCU caches and VPC buffers of 4 CCUs; 0023: **the private
  handle of newer Qualcomm grallocs** (no 'gmsm' magic: 0, 1, the flags with the same UBWC bit, the
  stride in bytes): before, a swapchain buffer kept an invalid modifier and Turnip laid the linear
  buffer out tiled with UBWC - a garbage picture and CCU write faults past the buffer's end
  (Nothing Phone 3, Android 17, 2026-11-06); 0024: **0017's rule stays off on the Adreno 8xx** -
  on the A825 command BOs placed below 4 GB (at 0x4000000 and up, right above the chip's UCHE GMEM
  aperture) stalled the CP: GPU timeouts every few seconds from the menus on, particle passes a
  hundred times slower, the device lost at level loads; with the BOs where KGSL puts them the same
  scene ran clean at 47-58 FPS (2026-11-07; `TU_IB_LOW4G=1` asks for the rule on an 8xx anyway);
  0025: **sysmem rendering by default on the Adreno 8xx** - GMEM (binned) rendering hangs the A825
  every 5-10 s (GPU timeouts, CP opcode errors) with this driver's entry and the community's values
  alike, with or without the low-4 GB rule, while sysmem runs clean at 40-58 FPS; `TU_A8XX_GMEM=1`
  lets the autotuner choose as before (other 8xx chips untested: the same default until they are);
  0026: **the command BOs behind a virtual BO alias and a cache of retired command BOs on the
  Adreno 8xx** (the idea of WinNative's A8xx patches - freeing command stream memory under the CP
  hangs the 8xx - written anew; A8xx and sysmem only; `TU_KGSL_IB_ALIAS=0` and `TU_KGSL_IB_CACHE=0`
  turn the parts off): kept in reserve on 2026-11-07 for the fatal "CP DDE BR opcode error |
  opcode=0x00000000" class, brought into the build on 2026-11-09 after the owner's Nothing Phone 3
  on 0.3.0 lost the device during a submit in U3's Cartagena market (findings 2026-11-09);
  untested on the phone yet.
  `experimental/` holds the A8xx experiments the build does not apply (`build_all.sh` takes
  `mesa-*.patch` of this directory only): the alias as written for Mesa main (0026 is its 26.2
  form) and the community's A825 tile and CCU cache values (GMEM rendering then runs on the A825,
  slower than sysmem). Both measured 2026-11-07.
  0018-0021 are the standalone Turnip package's (revvv991/turnip-a735: the top GPU clock switch,
  the descriptor buffer switch, upstream's A7xx varyings quirk, the KGSL node for DroidDeck), not
  applied here: one numbering for both.
  Experiments 0004-0011 are off by default; their variables stay for diagnostics.
